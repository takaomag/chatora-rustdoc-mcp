List the types that implement a Rust trait, within the crate that defines it, and what the trait asks of them: its items, each required or provided, whether it is dyn compatible, auto or unsafe, whether it is sealed, and whether it has negative impls. Answers questions such as "which types in tokio implement `AsyncRead`?", "what must I write to implement `Iterator`?" or "can I implement this trait outside its crate?".

`crate`, `version`⟨, `project`⟩ and `target` are as for `get_item`⟨; this workspace's crates are built here, private items too⟩. `path` is the trait (`tower::Service`, `std::iter::Iterator`); a trait re-exported from another crate is looked up there. `scope` is `crate`, the trait's crate⟨, or `dependency_graph`: every crate of the project's normal build that depends on it (for a trait of std's crates, those of std that depend on its crate and the whole build, nearest first), this workspace's built here, and its dependencies as built here before or on docs.rs, none built for this; each implementor then has its `crate` and `version`, and `provenance` its `crates_read` and the `sources` not read, or read as built before (`stale`), with `partial` when some were not⟩⟪; implementors in other crates are not listed, so call `list_impls` on a type to tell whether it implements the trait⟫. `limit` is the most implementors in a page (default 50, at most 200); pass the `cursor` of the previous page, with the same other arguments, for the next.

Returns `provenance` as `get_item` does, and `data`: the trait's `path` and `signature`, its `items` (`name`, `kind`, `provided`), `dyn_compatible`, `auto`, `unsafe`, `sealed` (why only its crate can implement it: a supertrait that is not public; its absence does not mean that others can), `has_negative_impls`, and `implementors`: each with the `type` as Rust writes it (`&T` and `[u8]` too), its `kind` (`trait`, `derive`, `blanket`, `negative` or `auto`) and its `header`. They are sorted by type⟨; across the graph, those of the trait's crate (of std's crates) first, then by crate and version⟩.
