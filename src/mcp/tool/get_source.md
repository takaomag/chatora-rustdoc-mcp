Show the source code of a Rust item, or of lines of a file of its crate: the definition of a function, a type, a trait or a macro, read from the crate's own sources. Answers questions such as "how is `tokio::sync::Mutex::lock` implemented?" or "what does lines 400-500 of `src/sync/mutex.rs` of tokio do?".

`crate`, `version`⟨, `project`⟩ and `target` are as for `get_item`. Pass either `path`, the item as for `get_item` (an item re-exported from another crate is read there), or `file`, a file of the crate relative to its package's root as `source_location.file` of `get_item` gives it, with `lines` (`434-466`, or one line; the whole file if left out). `context` adds that many lines before and after (default 0, at most 200): an item's lines leave out its doc comments and outer attributes. Only the crate's own files are read: those of its published `.crate`, never dotfiles nor files outside it. The sources of a release are fetched and unpacked the first time they are read.

Returns `provenance` as `get_item` does, and `data`: the item's `item` path and `kind`, its `file` and `lines`, `shown` (the lines returned) and `source`, the text of those lines; `path`, the real path of the file here, which a client can open; and `url`, the file on docs.rs. Invisible or control characters, as bidirectional controls, are shown as marks such as `<U+202E>`, with a warning. A source longer than 20,000 characters is cut at a line, with `truncated` and a hint to read the rest with `file` and `lines`. While the sources are fetched, `status` is `building`: call again. For the item's signature and documentation, call `get_item`.
