Read what changed in a Rust crate between two versions, from the changelog in its package (CHANGELOG, CHANGES, RELEASES, HISTORY or NEWS). Answers questions such as "what changed in tokio since the version we use?" or "what is new in axum 0.8?".

`crate` is as for `get_item`⟨, and `project` decides the version it uses⟩. `from` is the version after which to start, and `to` the last to tell; either may be a version in full or its first parts (`0.8` for every 0.8.x). Without `from`, the releases start ⟨after the version that the project uses, as its Cargo.lock has it (`provenance.from_project`), or ⟩at the first; without `to`, they go up to the latest release, with what is not released yet as `data.unreleased`. The changelog read is that of the `.crate` of the newest version of the range, which has the most releases⟨; of the project's other packages and of a crate of git, the one where it is, or its workspace's, from its first release unless `from` is given⟩.

Returns `provenance` as `resolve_version` does, of that version, `data.from` and `data.to` (that version), and `data.releases`, newest first, each with `version`, its `heading`, `date` and `yanked` when the heading tells, and its `text` in Markdown. A changelog without headings of versions comes as `data.text`, its lines. At most `limit` releases (default 10) and 20,000 characters come in a page: pass `next_cursor` as `cursor` for the rest; a release longer than a page is `truncated` at a line, and goes on in the next.

A crate without a changelog in its package is told so, with where else to look; many keep theirs in their repository's releases, which this tool does not read. `crate_metadata` tells the versions, and where a license, MSRV or edition changed.
