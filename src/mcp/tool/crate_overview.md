Give an overview of a Rust crate⟨ (this workspace's own, a dependency or any on docs.rs)⟩: the start of its root documentation and the headings of its sections, how many ⟪public ⟫items of each kind it has⟨ (public ones, but all of this workspace's)⟩, its top-level modules with the first sentence of each and the features they need, the other items at its root, and its features. Answers questions such as "what is this crate, and how is it organized?" before looking into its parts.

`crate`, `version`⟨, `project`⟩ and `target` are as for `get_item`⟨; this workspace's crates are built here, private items too⟩. `std`, `core`, `alloc`, `proc_macro` and `test` are those of a nightly toolchain. `detail` is `brief` (the first paragraph of the root's documentation), `normal` (the default: also the headings of its sections) or `full` (its whole text, section by section).

Returns `provenance` as `get_item` does, and `data`: the crate root's `path`, `docs`, `sections`, `counts` (items by kind), `modules`, `items` (at most 50, with `items_total`; `list_module` lists them all), and `features`: `all`, those that `default` enables, those that docs.rs did not build the documentation with (`not_in_docs`, whose items are missing)⟨, and with a version from the project, `enabled_in_project`⟩. For one module's contents call `list_module`; for one item, `get_item`.
