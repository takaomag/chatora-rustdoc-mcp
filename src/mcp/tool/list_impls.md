List the traits that a Rust type implements, sorted by how: its auto traits (`Send`, `Sync`, `Unpin`, `UnwindSafe`, `RefUnwindSafe`) with their conditions, the general traits of std that it has through blanket impls, and its other impls with their headers and where clauses. Answers questions such as "is `tokio::sync::Mutex` Send?", "under which conditions is `Arc<T>` Sync?" or "which traits does `MyType` implement?".

`crate`, `version`, `project` and `target` are as for `get_item`: without a version, the project's Cargo.lock decides it, or else the latest release; docs.rs has rustdoc JSON only for releases it built on or after 2025-05-23. `path` is the type: a struct, an enum, a union or a primitive type (`tokio::sync::Mutex`, `std::sync::Arc`); a type re-exported from another crate is looked up there. `limit` is the most impls in a page (default 50, at most 200); pass the `cursor` of the previous page, with the same other arguments, for the next.

Returns `provenance` as `get_item` does, and `data`: the type's `path` and `kind`, `non_exhaustive` and `has_private_fields`, `auto_traits` (each `yes`, `no`, or `if` with its conditions: to tell whether `Arc<Mutex<T>>` is Send, combine those of `Arc` and `Mutex`), `std_blanket` (`From`, `Into`, `Any` and the like, by name), and `impls`: each with its `kind` (`inherent`, `trait`, `derive`, `blanket` or `negative`), its `trait` and the trait's `crate` when that is another, and its `header`. Impls of this crate come first, then those of other crates, then those of std. The unstable auto traits `Freeze` and `UnsafeUnpin` are left out. For the methods, call `get_item` on each.
