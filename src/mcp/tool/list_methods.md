List the methods that can be called on a Rust type: its own (inherent) methods, the methods of the traits it implements, including the default ones it does not override, and those of blanket impls. Answers questions such as "what can I call on `tokio::sync::Mutex`?", "which trait gives `Duration` its `checked_add`?" or "does this need a `use`?". The methods of the type it dereferences to (`Vec<T>` to `[T]`) are not listed yet: `hints` names that type, to call this tool on again (a primitive such as `slice` or `str` with crate `core`, which defines most of its methods).

`crate`, `version`⟨, `project`⟩ and `target` are as for `get_item`⟨; this workspace's crates are built here, private items too⟩. `path` is the type: a struct, an enum, a union or a primitive type; a type re-exported from another crate is looked up there. `name` keeps methods whose name has this text, in any case. `from` keeps methods of one or more of `inherent`, `trait` and `blanket`. `expand_blanket` lists the methods of blanket impls one by one rather than by trait. `detail` is `brief` (the default: signature line and features) or `normal`. `limit` is the most methods in a page (default 50, at most 200); pass the `cursor` of the previous page, with the same other arguments, for the next.

Returns `provenance` as `get_item` does, and `data`: the type's `path` and `kind`, `methods` (each at `Type::method`, with `from`, its `trait`, `needs_use` when the trait is not in std's prelude, and `provided` when it is known whether the trait gives it a default body; default methods of another crate's trait come by name), `std_blanket` (the names of the methods that std's general traits give it, such as `to_string` and `into`), and without `expand_blanket`, `blanket` (each other blanket trait with its methods' names). Its own methods come first, then by trait.
